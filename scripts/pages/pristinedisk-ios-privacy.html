Pristine Disk for iPhone

Privacy policy

Effective November 5, 2026 · iPhone version

This policy describes Pristine Disk: AI Smart Cleaner for iPhone, provided by NetanelVision. Contact netanelvision@gmail.com with privacy questions. The Mac version has a separate policy.

Photos and videos

With your permission, the app accesses allowed photos, videos and their metadata to identify large videos, similar photos and pictures worth reviewing. Analysis includes image-quality measurements, image classification, text detection and barcode detection on your device. The app does not send your photos, recognized text, videos or scan results to NetanelVision or a cloud AI service. The photo scan does not request downloads of cloud-only originals for analysis.

Selected folders and file AI

The app reads names, paths, sizes, types and modification dates of accessible files in folders you select. Previews read supported file contents. If you request optional AI review, Apple’s on-device Foundation Models framework processes the filename, size and modification date and, for supported text formats, up to the first 4 KB of content. Other formats are assessed from metadata only. There is no cloud AI fallback. File samples and AI responses are not saved in a persistent analysis database.

Local analysis cache and preferences

To speed up repeat scans, the app stores photo identifiers, metadata fingerprints, small 32 × 32 grayscale image representations, suggestion reasons and video sizes in a local cache. It does not cache full-resolution photos, recognized text or barcode payloads. The cache is excluded from backups and uses iOS file protection. You can rebuild it from app settings. iOS may remove it to recover space. Sound preferences and the selected tab are stored locally.

Your permissions and choices

You can limit or revoke Photos access in iPhone Settings and choose which folders to open through the Files picker. When the app detects that all Photos permission has been removed, it clears its photo analysis cache. It prunes inaccessible items when reconciling a scan. Deleting the app removes its local app data, subject to copies retained by your device backup settings. Removing the app does not delete your original photos or files.

Deletion and cloud providers

The app requires preview and confirmation before deleting an item. Photos manages photo deletion, recovery and iCloud synchronization. File deletion uses your selected storage provider and may synchronize across devices. Provider recovery options vary and are not guaranteed. Apple and file providers operate under their own privacy policies. NetanelVision cannot remotely access or delete files held only on your device or in your provider account.

No advertising, tracking or account

The current iPhone app does not require a NetanelVision account and does not include advertising, third-party analytics SDKs or tracking. It does not upload your library, file contents or analysis results to our servers.

Support correspondence

If you contact us by email, we receive your email address, message and attachments you choose to send. We use them to respond and resolve your request, and retain correspondence as needed for the request and related follow-up. Email services process those messages. Avoid sending sensitive photos or files. You can contact us to request access, correction or deletion of your support correspondence, subject to applicable retention obligations.

This website

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may process IP addresses and technical request logs to operate and secure the service. The site does not add analytics or tracking cookies. Images and the optional preview video are hosted with the site, without third-party video embeds. See GitHub’s privacy statement and Apple’s privacy policy.

Questions and updates

For privacy requests, email netanelvision@gmail.com. We may update this policy when app behavior or our practices change. The effective date above identifies the current version.
