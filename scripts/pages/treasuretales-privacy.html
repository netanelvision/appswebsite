Privacy policy

Last updated: September 7, 2026

Your privacy matters to us. This policy explains what information may be collected and how it is used to operate TreasureTales by NetanelVision. Contact: netanelvision@gmail.com.

Information we collect

We may collect basic account information, location data while the app is in use, adventure progress and content you choose to upload.

How we use information

We use information to provide account access, show nearby adventures, save progress, operate routes and improve the app.

Camera and augmented reality

The camera provides the live AR experience. Camera imagery is not stored by us during normal use.

Location

Location helps show nearby adventures, recognize arrival at stops and activate stages. You can disable permission in device settings.

Sharing and retention

The app may use system services and sign-in providers. We do not sell personal information and retain it only as long as needed.

Children and safety

Children should use the app with adult supervision and remain aware of traffic and their surroundings.

Your choices

To delete your account, open Profile → Settings → Delete account in the app and confirm. This deletes your account, favorites, history, ratings, comments and hunts you created from the application database. Your hunts will no longer be available to other players. Apple accounts require confirmation with the same Apple account. No support email is required to initiate account deletion.

You can manage permissions in device settings and request information, correction or deletion by emailing netanelvision@gmail.com.

This website

This website has no analytics scripts or tracking cookies. Its host, GitHub Pages, may process technical request information to provide and secure the service.

Policy updates

We may update this policy as the service changes. The date above identifies the current revision.
