Start a photo scan

Open Photos in the app and tap Scan my photos. Allow access to your photo library, or choose limited access to scan only selected items. Review Large videos, Similar photos, Quality review and AI suggestions after scanning. Nothing is deleted during a scan.

Why are some photos missing?

Check the app’s Photos permission in iPhone Settings. With limited access, only the items you allow are visible. The scan does not download cloud-only media for analysis. Items that are not available locally may be skipped. Make needed originals available on the device in Photos, then scan again.

Scan files and folders

Open Files in the app, choose a folder using the system picker, then review Suggested or All files. iOS grants access only to selected locations. There is no permission to scan the root of all Files or other apps’ private storage. Providers may restrict folder access. Old files are identified by modification date; the app does not know when you last used them.

AI review and device compatibility

Photo analysis uses on-device image and text recognition. Optional file AI requires iOS 26 or later, an Apple Intelligence-compatible iPhone, and Apple Intelligence enabled with its model ready. It may be unavailable in some languages or regions. Metadata scanning remains available when file AI is unavailable. AI cannot know whether something matters to you; check every suggestion.

Similar photos and duplicates

Similar-photo groups are suggestions for comparison, not proof of identical content. The current iPhone version does not provide exact duplicate-file detection.

Repeat scans and cached analysis

Check for changes reuses saved analysis for unchanged photos. To start fresh, open the settings button and choose Rebuild photo cache. iOS may clear cached data when it needs space. The first scan after a rebuild can take longer.

Deletion and recovery

Open an item, load its preview and confirm deletion. Photos also asks for system confirmation. Photos manages Recently Deleted and any iCloud synchronization. File deletion may be permanent, and a provider may sync it to other devices; recovery is not guaranteed. Files whose contents changed since scanning must be rescanned. Keep independent backups of important items.

Sounds

Open the settings button to turn sound effects on or off. Sounds respect the iPhone’s silent setting.
