How do I get a reading?

Choose a birth date for each person, optionally enter your names, and tap “Reveal our connection.” After the animated reveal, you will see a compatibility score and a short romantic reading. Tap “Read the stars again” to return to the form.

How is the score calculated?

The app reduces each Gregorian birth date to a single numerology digit, then combines the two digits using a fixed formula. Names do not affect the score. Results are for entertainment and cannot predict whether a relationship will succeed.

How do I close the keyboard?

Tap Done above the keyboard, press its Done key, or scroll to dismiss it. Tapping Reveal also closes the keyboard.

How do I control the sound?

Tap the speaker button at the top of the app to mute or enable effects. This preference is remembered. Sounds also respect your device’s silent setting and volume.

What can I share?

“Share the magic” opens the system share sheet with the score, reading title, and entertainment notice. Choose the destination yourself.

Report a problem

Include your device model, iOS version, app version, and the steps that caused the problem. Please avoid sending real names or birth dates; they are not needed for ordinary support.
